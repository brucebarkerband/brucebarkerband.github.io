Easiest wayPost it in 3 steps

Tap the pink button above, tap Save Video, then follow the steps for where you want to post. A video you post yourself reaches more people than a shared link.

fFacebook post
  1. Open the Facebook app.
  2. Tap What's on your mind? and then Photo/video.
  3. Pick the Bruce Barker Band video, paste a caption from below, and tap Post.

Post it on your own page and the band page, then share it to the Frankfort and Clinton County community groups.

◎Instagram Reel
  1. Open Instagram and tap the + button.
  2. Choose Reel, then pick the Bruce Barker Band video.
  3. Tap Next, paste a caption, and tap Share.

Turn on "Also share to Facebook" if Instagram offers it, so one post covers both.

★Facebook or Instagram Story
  1. In either app, tap Create story or + and choose Story.
  2. Pick the video. It fills the whole screen because it's already vertical.
  3. Tap Your story to share. Stories last 24 hours, so post one again the week of the show.
✉︎Text it to friends
  1. Tap the pink button, then tap Messages to send the video itself.
  2. Or tap Text this page above to send this link, which works on any phone.
🙌Get others to share it
  1. Tag the Moose Lodge and Clinton Central athletics in your post so they can share it.
  2. Send this page to your bandmates and ask each of them to post it too.
  3. Ask friends to comment and share. More shares mean Facebook shows it to more people.
